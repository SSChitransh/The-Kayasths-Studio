Process

From first message to launch

  1. Enquiry and business requirements
  2. Scope and package confirmation
  3. Content, contact details and assets collected from you
  4. First website draft
  5. Consolidated revision rounds within your package
  6. Final approval, checks and launch
  7. Handover, plus a talk about maintenance and renewals
  8. You provide text, images and testimonials; we shape them

A revision round is one consolidated set of requested changes within the agreed scope. Extra pages, integrations, content writing or features outside scope may need a separate quote.
